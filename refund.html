Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

At NEPPLAY, we want every player to feel confident entering our tournaments. This Refund Policy explains when you're eligible for a refund and how to request one.

✅ Our Promise: If a tournament is cancelled by us, you get a 100% refund. No questions asked.

1. When You Get a Full Refund

  • Tournament cancelled by NEPPLAY before the match starts
  • Tournament postponed and you cannot attend the new date
  • Technical failure on our side that prevented your participation
  • Duplicate payment — if you accidentally paid twice for the same tournament
  • Payment verification failure — if we cannot verify your payment within 24 hours and you choose not to resubmit

2. When You Get a Partial Refund

  • Tournament format change — if we significantly change the format after you registered (e.g., Solo → Squad), you may request a refund within 1 hour of the change

3. When You Do NOT Get a Refund

  • No-show — you registered but didn't join the room on time
  • Disqualification — you violated tournament rules
  • Lost match — you played but didn't win (entry fee is for participation, not winning)
  • Internet issues — your connection failed during the match
  • Game technical issues — server lag, bugs, or crashes in the game itself
  • Change of mind — you simply decided not to play
  • Late arrival — you missed the start time

5. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email us at support@nepplay.com within 24 hours of the reason for the refund
  2. Include:
    • Your username and email
    • Tournament name and date
    • Transaction ID of your payment
    • Reason for the refund request
    • Screenshot evidence (if applicable)
  3. Wait for our reply — we respond within 3 business days
  4. If approved — refund is sent to your original payment method (eSewa/Khalti/IME Pay) within 5-7 business days

6. Refund Processing Time

  • Approval time: 1-3 business days after request
  • Money transfer time: 5-7 business days
  • Total expected: 1-2 weeks from request to money back in your wallet

7. Refund Method

Refunds are always sent back to the same payment method you used to pay (eSewa → eSewa, Khalti → Khalti, IME Pay → IME Pay).

We do not provide cash refunds or refunds to different accounts.

8. Chargebacks

Do not file chargebacks with your bank or payment provider. Contact us first — we resolve all legitimate refund requests. Fraudulent chargebacks will result in a permanent account ban.

9. Special Cases

9.1 Tournament Partially Played

If a tournament starts but is cancelled mid-way (e.g., after 1 of 3 matches due to technical failure), we may offer:

  • Full refund — if the winner cannot be determined
  • Partial refund — if some matches were played fairly and results are clear
  • Rescheduling — continue the tournament on a new date

9.2 Free Tournaments

Free tournaments have no refund policy (no payment was made). No-show players may be banned from future free tournaments.

For refund questions:
