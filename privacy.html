Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

🔒 Your privacy matters. We collect only what's necessary to run tournaments and never sell your data to anyone.

This Privacy Policy explains how NEPPLAY collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our Platform.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account info: Username, email address, password (encrypted), phone number
  • Game info: In-Game Name (IGN) for tournaments
  • Payment info: Transaction IDs from eSewa/Khalti/IME Pay, payment screenshots
  • Contact info: Any messages you send to our support team

1.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • IP address and general location (country/city level)
  • Pages visited, time spent on the Platform, and actions taken
  • Referring website or link

1.3 Information From Third Parties

  • Firebase Authentication (for login/session management)
  • Payment verification info from eSewa/Khalti/IME Pay

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Register you for tournaments
  • Verify payments and process payouts
  • Display your IGN and results on leaderboards
  • Notify you about tournament updates, results, and announcements
  • Prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse
  • Improve our Platform and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We only share data in these cases:

  • Public displays: Your username, IGN, and tournament results are visible to other users (leaderboards, winners page)
  • Payment processing: Transaction IDs may be shared with eSewa/Khalti/IME Pay for verification
  • Legal requirements: If required by Nepali law or court order
  • Business transfers: If NEPPLAY is sold or merged, user data may transfer to the new owner

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Where We Store Data

  • Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud) for user accounts, tournaments, registrations
  • Firebase Storage for payment screenshots
  • Our servers for session management

4.2 Security Measures

  • All passwords are hashed and encrypted
  • HTTPS/SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Firebase security rules restrict unauthorized access
  • Payment screenshots are stored securely and only visible to admins
  • Regular security audits and updates

4.3 Data Retention

  • Account data: retained as long as your account is active
  • Payment records: retained for 3 years (for tax/audit purposes)
  • Tournament results: retained permanently (part of platform history)
  • Deleted accounts: user data deleted within 30 days, except legally required records

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Opt out of non-essential communications
  • Complain to us about how we handle your data

To exercise any of these rights, email support@nepplay.com. We respond within 7 business days.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential cookies: Login sessions, security
  • Analytics cookies: Understanding how users use our Platform

You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work.

7. Children's Privacy

NEPPLAY is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16. If we discover such data, we will delete it immediately.

8. Third-Party Links

Our Platform may contain links to third-party sites (WhatsApp, Discord, game official sites). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Read their policies before sharing data.

9. International Transfers

Your data may be stored on servers outside Nepal (Google Cloud, primarily in Singapore/US). By using NEPPLAY, you consent to this transfer.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll notify you via email or on-platform notification for significant changes. Continued use = acceptance.

11. Contact Us

For privacy concerns:

  • 📧 Email: support@nepplay.com
  • 💬 WhatsApp: Community Group
